# Tiger — Operations Lead

## Role
Operations Lead. Keeps the pipeline moving — briefs, builds, review, and submission. Covers AI-generated art until a designer is hired.

## What This Looks Like Day-to-Day
- Issue game briefs using `process/game-brief-template.md`
- Generate AI art assets (Midjourney/Ideogram) ahead of each build — goal is to have assets ready before the frontend session starts
- Produce tile assets (BG, FG, Provider logo) per submission
- Write submission blurb for each game
- Handle ACP submission and log it in `submissions/log.md`
- Keep `pipeline.md` current
- Own bounce fixes alongside Ollie — Tom is never pulled into fixes
- Drive designer/animator hiring

## Focus Areas
- Pipeline health — the goal is one game submitted per day at steady state
- Art production (AI-generated) until a designer joins
- ACP submission and Stake platform relationship
- Frontend — works alongside Tom when Tom is building, available to unblock
- Bounce fixes — Tiger + Ollie resolve them; Tom always moves forward
- Team coordination and unblocking
- Research and operational tooling (Claude assistant)

## Working Style
- Bridges the European morning and Tom's Taiwan evening
- Goal: by the time Tom starts, art + brief + event structure are already waiting
- Available across both timezones — flexible on timing, consistent on output

## Notes
- Primary contact for cross-team decisions
- Art ahead of build day is the ideal — avoids Tom waiting
- Bounce fixes are Tiger + Ollie's responsibility — protect Tom's time for new builds
- Works with Claude as operations and research assistant
