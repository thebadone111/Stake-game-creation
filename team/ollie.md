# Ollie — Review Team Lead

## Role
Review Team Lead. Owns all QA and review before any game is submitted to Stake.

## Responsibilities
- Reviews every game against the review checklist before submission
- Bounces games back to Zacke with clear notes when issues are found
- Approves games for submission to Tiger
- Maintains and evolves the review checklist as standards develop

## Focus Areas
- Quality assurance
- Consistency of game mechanics and feel
- Compliance with Stake platform requirements
- Fast turnaround to maintain daily submission cadence

## Notes
- Gate between game build and Stake submission — nothing ships without Ollie's sign-off
- Should communicate rejections with specific, actionable feedback
