# Ollie — Review Lead

## Role
Review Lead. Owns QA and sign-off before any game is submitted to Stake.

## What This Looks Like Day-to-Day
- Review each game against `process/review-checklist.md` before submission
- Write clear, specific bounce notes when issues are found
- Approve games and hand off to Tiger for ACP submission
- Maintain and evolve the review checklist as standards develop
- Support Tiger on bounce fix work — especially asset and visual issues

## Focus Areas
- Quality assurance — catch issues before Stake reviewers do
- Compliance with Stake platform requirements
- Clear, fast feedback to keep the pipeline moving

## Working Style
- Works European mornings
- Goal is to turn reviews around the same morning they land
- Reviews the Cloudflare Pages preview URL — no local setup needed

## Notes
- Gate between build and submission — nothing ships without sign-off
- Feedback should be specific and actionable, not just "fix this"
- Can support Tiger on asset-level bounce fixes when available
